# Q051 — Longest Active Streak

## Business Context

Loyalty program design wants to understand consistency of engagement,
not just total activity — a customer who orders every single month for
a year is a different kind of valuable than one with the same total
order count spread erratically across two years.

## Business Question

For each customer, find the longest streak of consecutive calendar
months in which they placed at least one completed order.

## Data Available

orders

## Attempt

This question does not tell you the technique. Work through it
yourself first:

- What does one output row represent?
- What does "a streak" mean in terms of rows you'd need to compare to
  each other?
- What would break a streak — and how do you detect that with data
  that only has rows for months where activity *did* happen?
