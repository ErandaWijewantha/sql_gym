# Q060 — Support Friction and Going Quiet

## Business Context

Customer success suspects that slow support ticket resolution might be
driving customers away, but wants evidence before making the case for
more support headcount.

## Business Question

Compare the subsequent purchasing behavior of customers whose most
recent support ticket took a long time to resolve against those whose
most recent ticket was resolved quickly.

## Data Available

support_tickets
orders
order_items
customers

## Attempt

This question does not tell you the technique. Work through it
yourself first:

- What does "subsequent purchasing behavior" mean, precisely — and
  relative to what date, for each customer?
- What two groups are you actually comparing, and what determines which
  group a customer falls into?
- What are at least two different explanations (besides "slow support
  causes churn") for why these two groups might show different
  purchasing behavior, even if support speed had no real effect at all?
