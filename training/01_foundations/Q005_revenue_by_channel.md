# Q005 — Revenue by Sales Channel

## Business Context

Leadership wants to know how gross sales split between the physical
store network and the online channel for completed orders.

## Business Question

What is the total gross revenue (quantity x unit price, before
discounts) generated by each sales_channel, considering only completed
orders?

## Data Available

orders
order_items

## Think Before SQL

1. What does one output row represent?
2. What is the metric, precisely — does "gross revenue" include or
   exclude discounts? Which columns tell you the answer either way?
3. Why does this question restrict to "completed" orders specifically —
   what would including cancelled/pending/refunded orders do to the
   number, and would it still mean "revenue"?
4. Which table holds sales_channel, and which holds quantity/unit_price?
5. What kind of JOIN connects them, and at what grain does the join
   output live before you aggregate?
6. What expression computes one line item's contribution to revenue?
7. What clause turns per-line-item amounts into a per-channel total?
8. Could this JOIN duplicate any revenue if handled carelessly? Why or
   why not, specifically for orders → order_items?
9. Roughly what order of magnitude would you expect gross revenue to be,
   given ~120K orders averaging a few thousand LKR each? Use that as a
   sanity check.
10. Is "gross revenue" here the same thing an accountant would call
    "revenue"? What's missing?
