# Q001 — Premium Product Listing

## Business Context

The merchandising team is reviewing the high end of the catalog before
setting next quarter's premium-tier marketing push.

## Business Question

List every active product priced above LKR 100,000, from most to least
expensive.

## Data Available

products

## Think Before SQL

1. What does one output row represent?
2. What is the business metric (if any) — or is this just a filtered list?
3. Which column expresses "priced"? Is that the same as what the customer
   pays?
4. What does "active" filter out, and why would merchandising care?
5. What determines the order of the output?
6. Do you need any other table for this question?
7. Are we comparing rows to each other, or filtering rows independently?
8. What SQL clauses do you expect to need?
9. What edge cases exist (ties in price, NULL prices)?
10. How would you sanity-check the result once you have it?
